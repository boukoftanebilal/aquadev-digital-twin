# AquaDev shrimp farm — Innovation Oasis monitoring platform

A browser platform for the AquaDev grow-out pilot at Innovation Oasis (zone 15 of the campus; plot 8,
Al Sajaa, Al Fou'ah). Two organisations sign in:

- **Aqua Development** enters one data round every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 Gulf Standard Time):
  a row per tank, the farm meters, and any stocking or harvest.
- **Innovation Oasis** monitors the farm on the digital twin and the KPI pages, and issues PDF reports.

## What is in it

| Page | Who | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Update data | AD | The 6-hourly round: 28 tank rows, farm meters, stocking and harvest events, note. Paste from Excel, limits checked as you type. |
| Round history | AD | The last 28 rounds: submitted, due, overdue or missing; amend any of them. |
| Digital twin | both | 3D model to scale: the vaulted farm hall with its roof, skylights and steel frame, 28 tanks, treatment columns, service rooms, the water treatment block, and the whole Innovation Oasis campus around it (trial plots, main building, greenhouses, support buildings, roads). |
| Tanks, Water, Production and KPIs, Energy and climate, Alarms and log, Facility | IO | Every tank against its limits, the workbook KPIs month by month, energy, hall climate, the event log, the drawing register. |
| Reports | IO | Status, daily, weekly and monthly KPI reports as PDF, with both logos and a picture of the twin. |
| Data file | both | Download or load every round and event as one file. |

## Read this before relying on it

- **There is no shared database yet.** Access codes and entered rounds are stored in the browser that
  created them. A round entered on one computer reaches another only through the Data file page.
  `data.js` (`load`/`save`) and `auth.js` (`signIn`) are the two places to connect a real service.
- **Sample history** fills the platform until the first real round is submitted. It is generated by
  `sim.js`, tuned to the KPI workbook targets, and is marked "sample" wherever it appears, including in reports.
- The sign-in keeps casual visitors out; it is not security. The page and its code are public.

## Files

`model.js` farm geometry and KPI targets from the drawings and workbook · `site.js` campus geometry from
master plan A012 · `twin.js` 3D scene (three.js from jsDelivr) · `data.js` rounds, events and everything
derived from them · `sim.js` sample-history generator · `auth.js` sign-in · `entry.js` data round ·
`views.js`, `app.js`, `ui.js`, `charts.js` pages · `report.js` PDF reports (jsPDF from jsDelivr).

No build step. Serve the folder with any static web server, or publish it with GitHub Pages.

## Assumptions to confirm

1. Tank numbering 1–28 runs row by row from grid A/1; the drawings do not number the tanks.
2. The structures at campus zones 2 and 3 are unlabelled on the master plan and are modelled as greenhouses.
   Plot contents and the uses of the smaller support buildings are read from shapes and room labels.
3. One treatment column and one pump per tank, from the renders. Water treatment Tank 1 = raw, Tank 2 = treated.
4. Water-quality limits are typical *P. vannamei* values pending the contract list the workbook refers to.
5. Stocking of 34,000 juveniles per tank per cycle (612 kg/cycle at 70 % survival and 25 g).
