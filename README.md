# AquaDev shrimp farm — Innovation Oasis monitoring platform

A browser platform for the AquaDev grow-out pilot at Innovation Oasis (zone 15 of the campus; plot 8,
Al Sajaa, Al Fou'ah). Everyone signs in with a username and password. Three roles:

- **Super admin** creates the users, sets and resets their passwords, disables or deletes accounts,
  and can do everything the other two roles can.
- **Aqua Development** enters one data round every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 Gulf Standard Time):
  a row per tank, the farm meters, and any stocking or harvest. AD also records and resolves alarms.
- **Innovation Oasis** monitors the farm on the digital twin and the KPI pages, acknowledges alarms,
  and issues PDF reports.

The first person to open the platform on a computer creates the super admin account. There are no
preset usernames or passwords.

**Alarms are recorded by hand.** Nothing raises an alarm automatically: AD records what happened, where,
how severe it is and what was done, then marks it resolved. Readings outside their limits are still
highlighted on the Tanks and Water pages, as a prompt, but they are not alarms.

## What is in it

| Page | Who | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Update data | AD | The 6-hourly round: 28 tank rows, farm meters, stocking and harvest events, note. Paste from Excel, limits checked as you type. |
| Round history | AD | The last 28 rounds: submitted, due, overdue or missing; amend any of them. |
| Digital twin | both | 3D model to scale: the vaulted farm hall with its roof, skylights and steel frame, 28 tanks, treatment columns, service rooms, the water treatment block, and the whole Innovation Oasis campus around it (trial plots, main building, greenhouses, support buildings, roads). |
| Alarms and log | all | Record an alarm (AD), acknowledge it (IO), resolve it with a note (AD); alarm history and the event log. |
| Tanks, Water, Production and KPIs, Energy and climate, Facility | IO | Every tank against its limits, the workbook KPIs month by month, energy, hall climate, the drawing register. |
| Reports | IO | Status, daily, weekly and monthly KPI reports as PDF, with both logos, a picture of the twin and the alarms of the period. |
| Users | super admin | Create users with a role and password, reset passwords, disable, enable and delete accounts. |
| My account | all | Change your own password. |
| Data file | all | Download or load every round, event and alarm as one file. A super admin's file also carries the accounts. |

## Read this before relying on it

- **There is no shared database yet.** Accounts, rounds and alarms are stored in the browser that
  created them. They reach another computer only through the data file (Data file page, or
  "Load its data file" on the first-use screen). `data.js` (`load`/`save`) and `auth.js`
  (`readUsers`/`writeUsers`) are the two places to connect a real service.
- **Sample history** fills the platform until the first real round is submitted. It is generated by
  `sim.js`, tuned to the KPI workbook targets, and is marked "sample" wherever it appears, including in reports.
- Passwords are never stored: each account keeps a salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 hash. The checks still run in
  the browser, so the sign-in keeps people in their role but is not server-grade security. The page and
  its code are public.

## Files

`model.js` farm geometry and KPI targets from the drawings and workbook · `site.js` campus geometry from
master plan A012 · `twin.js` 3D scene (three.js from jsDelivr) · `data.js` rounds, events and everything
derived from them · `sim.js` sample-history generator · `auth.js` accounts, roles and sign-in · `entry.js` data round ·
`views.js`, `app.js`, `ui.js`, `charts.js` pages · `report.js` PDF reports (jsPDF from jsDelivr).

No build step. Serve the folder with any static web server, or publish it with GitHub Pages.

## Assumptions to confirm

1. Tank numbering 1–28 runs row by row from grid A/1; the drawings do not number the tanks.
2. The structures at campus zones 2 and 3 are unlabelled on the master plan and are modelled as greenhouses.
   Plot contents and the uses of the smaller support buildings are read from shapes and room labels.
3. One treatment column and one pump per tank, from the renders. Water treatment Tank 1 = raw, Tank 2 = treated.
4. Water-quality limits are typical *P. vannamei* values pending the contract list the workbook refers to.
5. Stocking of 34,000 juveniles per tank per cycle (612 kg/cycle at 70 % survival and 25 g).
